03 / How it works

No scraping. No paid APIs. Just public job feeds, normalized and ranked.

STEP / 01

Aggregate

Every hour, fetch JSON from three free public job APIs. No scraping, no rate limits, no ToS violations.

STEP / 02

Normalize

Map each source's schema into one canonical job object. Score by recency, salary disclosure, and role-tag match.

STEP / 03

Publish

Static-generated listings, schema.org/JobPosting markup, individual pages per role for SEO. Free hosting on GitHub Pages.
